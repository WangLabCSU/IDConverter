# IDConverter: Convert Identifiers in Biological Databases

Identifiers in biological databases connect different levels of
metadata, phenotype data or genotype data. This tool is designed to
easily convert identifiers within or between different biological
databases (Wang, Shixiang, et al. (2021)
[doi:10.1371/journal.pgen.1009557](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1009557)
).
